How it's made

  • Terrain: 20 m controlled CTX DTM, Mars 2020 TRN (USGS Astrogeology).
  • Texture: controlled MRO CTX and HiRISE mosaics of Jezero (Mars Trek).
  • Night cold: THEMIS night IR (Mars Trek), relative.
  • Radiation: modeled from the average measured by RAD (Curiosity), visible sky and altitude.
