How it works
  • Vehicle: NASA SEV-type pressurized rover · 8 km/h on flat ground · max slope 18° · 72 h · 60 kWh battery (assumed values).
  • A* search over the CTX DTM: 20 m cells, 8 directions.
  • Cells whose slope (at ~60 m scale) exceeds the vehicle maximum are off-limits.
  • Speed depends on segment slope (on foot: Tobler's function) and terrain roughness.
  • Fastest: minimizes time. Safest: penalizes slope and roughness. Least energy: rolling resistance and climb.
  • Rocks and steps under ~20 m are invisible to it: a real sortie would need the 1 m HiRISE DTM.
